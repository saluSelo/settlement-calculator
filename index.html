The Equation, Explained
ρₖ  =  Ƥ1  ×  [  P  +  Σⱼ ( P × αⱼₖ )  ]
Settlement of the reference pile (k) = flexibility × (its own load + the load-weighted interaction from every other pile j in the group)
ρₖ
Settlement of the reference pile same length unit as Ƥ1's numerator
The final answer. Shown as "Result — ρₖ" after you calculate.
Ƥ1
Pile flexibility length per Ton — e.g. in/ton or m/ton
Settlement of a single pile under one unit of load, from a real load test (e.g. "0.6 in under 50 tons" → Ƥ1 = 0.012). PLACEHOLDER until the client provides their own test result.
P
Load per pile Tons
The load applied to each pile. In this app, P = total weight of all currently-active tanks (W = v × h, summed).
j, k, n
Pile indices — (count)
k = the reference pile whose settlement we're solving for. j = every other pile in that group. Σ adds up each one's contribution.
αⱼₖ
Interaction factor dimensionless, 0 – 1
How much pile j's load affects pile k's settlement, based on spacing. Looked up from the Poulos & Davis chart using s/d and K.
s/d
Spacing ratio dimensionless
Center-to-center spacing between two piles ÷ pile diameter. Comes from the selected Pile Group Layout's geometry data.
K
Pile-soil stiffness ratio (Ep/Es) dimensionless
Looked up automatically from Soil Type + Pile Material.
v, h, W
Tank volume, height, weight v: m³ · h: m · W: Tons
W = v × h per tank. Active tanks sum to the per-pile load P used above.
